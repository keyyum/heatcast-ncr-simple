# The final paper

* `HeatCast_NCR_IMRaD_Paper.docx` and `HeatCast_NCR_IMRaD_Paper.pdf`: the IMRaD paper in the IEEE conference layout (two columns,
  8 pages of body text and references, then the appendices: contribution record, ownership and authorship declaration, data
  dictionary, sample inputs and outputs). Both are exported from the group's Google Doc, which stays the editable copy.
* `figures/`: Figs. 1 to 8 at one-column width, made by `python src/paper_figures.py` from the data and the app screenshots.

Every number in the paper comes from the notebooks and scripts in this repository: `notebooks/01_eda.ipynb` to
`05_final_test.ipynb`, `src/compare_models.py` (Tables III to V) and `app/sample_inputs_outputs.py` (Table IX).

Before submission, export the two files again once every member has confirmed Appendix A and the declaration in Appendix B
is signed (Google Docs: File, Download, .docx and .pdf).
